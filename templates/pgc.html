PROJECT MANAGEMENT

Project General Context

This section presents the context, problem, research question, justification, and objectives of the Predictive Maintenance System project. It establishes the foundation for the development of a software solution supported by Machine Learning.

01 · PROBLEM STATEMENT

Identification of the Problem

Companies use different devices and equipment to carry out their daily activities. However, keeping track of maintenance dates, service history, and equipment conditions can become difficult when this information is managed manually or stored in different places.

Inadequate maintenance management can lead to missed inspections, unexpected equipment failures, operational interruptions, and increased maintenance costs.

For this reason, a system is needed to centralize equipment information, record maintenance activities, monitor service schedules, and provide information that helps identify possible equipment failures.

02 · RESEARCH QUESTION

Research Question

How can a predictive maintenance system be developed to manage company devices, monitor maintenance schedules, and estimate failure risk based on maintenance history and time elapsed since the last service?

03 · JUSTIFICATION

Why Is This Project Important?

The development of a predictive maintenance system is important because it provides companies with a centralized tool to manage their equipment and maintenance information.

The system will allow users to register devices, record maintenance activities, review service history, and receive alerts when maintenance is approaching or overdue.

In addition, the integration of a Machine Learning model will make it possible to analyze available data and identify patterns associated with equipment failure risk. This information can support maintenance planning and decision-making.

The project is feasible because it can be developed using accessible programming tools, a database, and publicly available datasets for experimentation and model evaluation.

04 · PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Project Objectives

General Objective

To develop a predictive maintenance system using Machine Learning that enables companies to register devices, manage maintenance schedules, and estimate equipment failure risk based on available data.

SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
OBJECTIVE 01

Identify Requirements

Identify the functional requirements needed for device registration and maintenance management.

OBJECTIVE 02

Design the Database

Design and implement a database to store device information, maintenance dates, and service history.

OBJECTIVE 03

Develop the ML Model

Develop a Machine Learning model to analyze available data and estimate equipment failure risk.

OBJECTIVE 04

Implement Alerts

Implement an alert system based on the time elapsed since the last maintenance and the estimated failure risk.

OBJECTIVE 05

Evaluate the System

Evaluate the software through functional tests for registration, maintenance tracking, alerts, and predictions.

Project Approach

The system combines two complementary functions: scheduled maintenance monitoring, based on maintenance dates and elapsed time, and failure risk estimation, supported by a Machine Learning model. These functions provide different types of information to support equipment management.
