CRISP-DM · PHASE 01

Business Understanding

Understanding the business problem, defining project objectives and establishing the scope of the Predictive Maintenance System.

This phase establishes the foundation of the project by identifying the maintenance challenges, the purpose of the proposed solution and the expected results.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Context

Maintenance management plays an important role in ensuring equipment availability, operational continuity and efficient use of resources. Traditional maintenance approaches may rely on scheduled inspections or corrective actions after a failure has occurred.

Predictive maintenance introduces a data-driven approach that uses equipment information and analytical models to estimate the possibility of future failures. This allows maintenance teams to obtain additional information when planning maintenance activities.

In this context, the Predictive Maintenance System project proposes a web-based platform that integrates equipment management, maintenance records, alerts, predictions and information visualization.

Project focus: Develop a centralized web application that combines maintenance management functionalities with machine learning predictions to support maintenance-related decision-making.

02 / PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION

Business Problem

Unexpected equipment failures can generate operational interruptions, additional maintenance expenses and difficulties in planning maintenance activities. When maintenance information is fragmented or when failures are addressed only after they occur, organizations may have limited information to anticipate potential problems.

Main Challenges

Unexpected Failures

Equipment breakdowns can occur without sufficient warning, affecting availability and operational continuity.

Maintenance Organization

Equipment information and maintenance history need to be organized to facilitate monitoring and follow-up.

Limited Predictive Information

Traditional maintenance records alone may not provide an estimate of failure risk based on equipment operating variables.

Decision-Making

Maintenance planning benefits from having equipment information, alerts and analytical results available in one place.

Problem Statement

The project addresses the need for a centralized maintenance management solution that organizes equipment information and provides predictive insights to help identify potential failure risks and support maintenance planning.

03 / PROJECT OBJECTIVES

General Objective

To develop a web-based Predictive Maintenance System that integrates equipment management, maintenance records, automated alerts and machine learning predictions to support equipment monitoring and maintenance decision-making.

Specific Objectives

  • Develop an equipment management module to register and organize relevant equipment information.
  • Implement maintenance record management to maintain a history of equipment maintenance activities.
  • Integrate an alert mechanism to support maintenance monitoring and follow-up.
  • Incorporate a machine learning model capable of estimating failure probability using the variables supported by the model.
  • Develop a dashboard to display relevant information and system indicators.
  • Integrate the different modules into a centralized web application.
04 / PROJECT STAKEHOLDERS

Stakeholders and Users

The system is intended to support people involved in equipment monitoring and maintenance activities. The following roles represent potential users of the proposed platform.

Stakeholder Interest or Responsibility
System Administrator Manage system information and coordinate access to its functionalities.
Maintenance Personnel Review equipment information, maintenance history and alerts.
Equipment Managers Monitor equipment status and consult maintenance information.
Decision-Makers Use available system information to support maintenance planning.

Note: These are proposed stakeholder roles for the project and do not necessarily represent implemented user accounts or permissions.

05 / PROJECT DEFINITION

Project Scope

Included Functionalities

  • Equipment registration and information management.
  • Maintenance record registration and consultation.
  • Maintenance alerts and status monitoring.
  • Failure probability estimation through the integrated machine learning model.
  • Dashboard visualization of system information.
  • Integration of the functionalities into a web-based application.

Technical Scope and Considerations

The project uses a machine learning approach based on the AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset. This dataset contains industrial equipment operating variables and failure-related information.

The prediction module must be interpreted according to the variables and equipment domain represented in the training dataset. Its predictions should not be assumed to be validated for unrelated equipment categories without additional training and validation.

Important limitation: The availability of equipment registration functionality does not automatically mean that the machine learning model has been validated for every registered equipment type.

06 / EXPECTED VALUE

Expected Benefits

Centralized Information

Organize equipment information and maintenance records in a single platform.

Maintenance Monitoring

Facilitate the review of maintenance activities and relevant alerts.

Predictive Insights

Provide model-based estimates that can complement maintenance information.

Decision Support

Present organized information to help users plan and prioritize maintenance tasks.

07 / PROJECT EXPECTATIONS

Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when the developed system demonstrates the following capabilities:

  • The application allows equipment information to be registered and consulted.
  • Maintenance records can be managed and reviewed.
  • The alert mechanism supports the identification of maintenance-related events.
  • The prediction module processes supported input variables and returns a model result.
  • The dashboard presents relevant system information in an organized way.
  • The main application modules operate together as part of the same system.

These are qualitative project acceptance criteria. Model performance must be evaluated separately using appropriate validation metrics.

08 / PHASE CONCLUSION

Business Understanding Summary

The Business Understanding phase establishes the purpose and direction of the Predictive Maintenance System project. It identifies the need to organize equipment and maintenance information while incorporating machine learning predictions as an additional source of decision support.

Based on this understanding, the next CRISP-DM phase focuses on Data Understanding, where the dataset, its variables, structure and characteristics will be examined.
